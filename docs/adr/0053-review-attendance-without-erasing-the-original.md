# Review Attendance without erasing the original

A Student may open one Attendance Review Request per Booking within seven days of Attendance publication, with an optional 500-character explanation. The original outcome remains effective while pending. A Platform Administrator reasonedly upholds or corrects it, retaining both outcomes and auditing the request and decision; the Student receives the effective outcome and a concise Student-visible rationale, while the Teacher sees the result but not private administrator notes. Corrections recompute reporting and Sponsorship snapshots and create or remove Lesson Unit Completion only when no other Attended Booking supports that completion. Credit is unaffected unless an administrator separately issues a reasoned adjustment for platform failure.
