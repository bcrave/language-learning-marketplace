# Snapshot Organization-visible progress

Sponsorship captures aggregate Course Progress at its start, updates a current aggregate while active, and freezes an ending snapshot. Reports show counts, percentage, snapshot time, and units completed during Sponsorship but conceal identities of units completed beforehand and all later progress. Attendance and completion attribution follows the Class Session occurrence, regardless of Booking time or the origin of the fungible credit used. Cohort aggregation additionally requires active Cohort membership at occurrence time. These snapshots prevent later Student activity or curriculum changes from silently rewriting historical employer reports.
