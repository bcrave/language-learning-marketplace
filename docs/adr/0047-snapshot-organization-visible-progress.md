# Snapshot Organization-visible progress

Sponsorship captures aggregate Course Progress at its start, updates a current aggregate while active, and freezes an ending snapshot. Reports show counts, percentage, snapshot time, and units completed during Sponsorship but conceal identities of units completed beforehand and all later progress. Attendance and completion attribution follows the Class Session occurrence, regardless of Booking time or the origin of the fungible credit used. Cohort aggregation additionally requires active Cohort membership at occurrence time. The boundary freezes its time scope and active Lesson Unit denominator, but a later Attendance correction revises completion facts attributed to that period against the frozen denominator. This permits known errors to be corrected without allowing later Student activity or curriculum changes to silently rewrite historical employer reports.
