# Grant eight monthly Sponsorship credits

Sponsorship grants eight Class Credits at Student acceptance and eight on each monthly anniversary under the same original-day and short-month convention as the personal plan. Acceptance, Sponsorship creation, and the initial ledger grant commit in one transaction so the reporting relationship and promised benefit cannot diverge. Grants stop after termination and are skipped without backfill during User Suspension. Permanent ledger references retain Organization and Sponsorship provenance, but credits remain fungible and Student-owned. A Student may also hold the personal subscription. Organization reports expose grants from their Sponsorship but not total balance or per-Booking source; only Platform Administrators issue reasoned adjustments.
