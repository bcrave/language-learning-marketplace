# Structure and explicitly publish Learning Feedback

One Learning Feedback record per Attended Booking may identify up to three strengths and three focus areas from listening, reading, spoken interaction, spoken production, writing, vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation, plus optional 500-character observations and next-practice text. At least one field is required for submission. Drafts remain teacher-private, submitted content is Student-visible and editable only through 48 hours after the session, and every revision is audited without copying comments. Feedback has no numeric score or automatic Placement effect. Administrators may inspect but only redact inappropriate content with a concise reason visible to the Teacher and, for already-submitted feedback, the Student; redaction history remains and administrators cannot rewrite the teacher's guidance.
