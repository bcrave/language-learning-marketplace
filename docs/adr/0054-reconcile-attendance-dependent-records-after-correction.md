# Reconcile attendance-dependent records after correction

When Attended changes to No-show, the system recomputes the Lesson Unit Completion and Lesson Material access, voids related Learning Feedback and Session Rating without deletion, hides them from ordinary Student views, and excludes the rating from analytics. When No-show changes to Attended, it establishes completion and access, opens a seven-day rating window and a 48-hour feedback window from correction, and temporarily restores the assigned Teacher's access only to that Booking's feedback context. The Student is notified of every correction, and the assigned Teacher is notified when another actor made it; a Teacher does not receive a redundant notification for their own correction. A later correction that restores eligibility may reactivate preserved records rather than recreating them.
