# Export current effective reporting snapshots

Organization Manager and Platform Administrator Report Exports use one asynchronous flow that captures current effective reporting facts in a transactionally consistent snapshot when generation starts. Ordinary CSVs expose correction markers rather than rewriting history invisibly, while a separately authorized correction-history export exposes prior and current values without actors or reasons; investigative identity and rationale remain in the filtered, append-only Audit Log. That separate authorization is the Platform Administrator Role Assignment: an Organization Manager's authority covers its sponsored Students' attendance and curriculum completion, and a superseded value is investigative material about how a fact came to be rather than one of those facts, so authority over the ordinary export never carries into prior values. Exports require an explicit date range interpreted in the requester's Display Time Zone, bounded to at most 12 months and 25,000 rows; they never truncate, use a stable locale-independent schema, expire after 24 hours, and recheck authorization during generation and download. Historical as-of regeneration is deliberately excluded: an Audit Entry names the immutable Report Export record, which retains the acting role, filters, schema version, completion time, row count, and digest but never the exported content — the Audit Entry itself stays inside the fields ADR 0004 allows, so a later export truthfully reflects subsequent valid corrections.
