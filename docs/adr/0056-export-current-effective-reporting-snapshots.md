# Export current effective reporting snapshots

Organization Manager and Platform Administrator Report Exports use one asynchronous flow that captures current effective reporting facts in a transactionally consistent snapshot when generation starts. Ordinary CSVs expose correction markers rather than rewriting history invisibly, while a separately authorized correction-history export exposes prior and current values without actors or reasons; investigative identity and rationale remain in the filtered, append-only Audit Log. Exports require an explicit date range interpreted in the requester's Display Time Zone, bounded to at most 12 months and 25,000 rows; they never truncate, use a stable locale-independent schema, expire after 24 hours, and recheck authorization during generation and download. Historical as-of regeneration is deliberately excluded: an Audit Entry retains the acting role, filters, schema version, completion time, row count, and digest but not exported content, so a later export truthfully reflects subsequent valid corrections.
