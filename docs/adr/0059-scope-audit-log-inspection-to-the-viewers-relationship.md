# Scope Audit Log inspection to the viewer's relationship

The Audit Log opens the append-only Audit Entries of [ADR 0004](0004-immutable-audit-history.md) to two acting roles and no others. A Platform Administrator reads marketplace-wide. An Organization Manager reads the acting record of its own Organization's managers — who granted, renamed, ended, or exported what — and nothing else. That narrower scope is deliberate: an Organization's disclosed reporting covers a sponsored Student's attendance and curriculum progress, so surfacing that Student's own Bookings, cancellations, credits, feedback, or denied reads through an audit surface would widen the Sponsorship boundary of [ADR 0048](0048-require-student-acceptance-for-sponsorship.md) without the Student ever accepting it. Scoping by acting manager instead of by sponsored subject keeps the Organization's own accountability legible while making that leak structurally impossible: a Student is never an actor inside the scope. Students and Teachers reach no Audit Log at all; their attempt is a denied sensitive read and is itself audited. Marketplace-wide authority is decided before Organization membership, so an administrator who also manages an Organization never silently narrows.

A successful read of the Audit Log records no Audit Entry. ADR 0004 audits mutations, denied sensitive reads, and background actions, and a log that recorded every inspection of itself would bury the history an investigation came for under the investigation. An export is treated differently for the same reason a Report Export download is: the entries leave the platform, so both the released file and the refusal are audited.

Export is synchronous, bounded, and refused rather than truncated past 5,000 Audit Entries, and reads are cursor-paginated in groups of 20 over a range of at most 12 months read in the viewer's Display Time Zone. An Audit Log export is not a [Report Export](0056-export-current-effective-reporting-snapshots.md): it carries no reporting facts, needs no consistent-snapshot generation, and is small enough per row that the asynchronous request-generate-expire lifecycle would add machinery without adding safety. Its schema carries opaque actor and target identities, the acting role, the operation, the outcome, the reason code, the time, and the correlation identifier — the same fields the read surface projects, because an Audit Entry has no other fields to give.

Retention runs as one scheduled sweep that prepares the coming months' partitions before dropping every month whose last possible instant has left the 90-day window. Dropping a whole partition is the only removal the Audit Log permits; the append-only trigger still refuses an update or a single-row delete, to the retention worker as much as to any Platform Administrator, so retention can never become a way to rewrite retained history.
