# Keep a redacted, append-only Audit Log

The platform records every authenticated mutation whether successful or denied, every denied sensitive-data read, and every background action as an Audit Entry. Each Audit Entry contains the actor or system identity, acting role, operation, target identifiers, timestamp, outcome, reason, and correlation or idempotency key, but excludes secrets and sensitive content such as full feedback text. Audit Entries are immutable even to Platform Administrators; corrections append new entries and successful ordinary reads are omitted initially. Project Owner workflows that change application state record an Audit Entry with a redacted owner-operation or system identity, safe reason, outcome, and workflow correlation; deployment approvals, diagnostics reads, secret access, and provider-console actions remain in private provider histories rather than being copied into the application Audit Log. The public portfolio retains complete monthly Audit Log partitions for 90 days, after which a scheduled policy may drop an entire expired partition, but no actor may selectively alter or delete Audit Entries. Filtered Audit Log export respects the same authorization boundary.
