# Keep a redacted, append-only audit history

The platform records every authenticated mutation whether successful or denied, every denied sensitive-data read, and every background action. Entries contain the actor or system identity, acting role, operation, target identifiers, timestamp, outcome, reason, and correlation or idempotency key, but exclude secrets and sensitive content such as full feedback text. Audit entries are immutable even to Platform Administrators; corrections append new entries and successful ordinary reads are omitted initially. The public portfolio retains monthly append-only partitions for 90 days, after which a scheduled policy may drop an entire expired partition but no actor may selectively alter or delete entries. Filtered export respects the same authorization boundary.
