# Maintain time-relevant demo fixtures

The synthetic environment combines a nightly Canonical Data Rebuild orchestrated outside Railway by GitHub Actions with rolling showcase fixtures maintained hourly by the Graphile Worker. Canonical data covers curriculum, roles, credits, history, reporting, capacity, waitlists, substitutions, cancellation boundaries, and daylight-saving examples. Rolling, clearly labeled sessions touch at their boundaries and keep the shared Student and Teacher identities continuously associated with a current or immediately upcoming simulated classroom. The showcase uses the real clock and ordinary scheduling invariants rather than a client-visible fake-time mode, and its transient business records disappear during the Canonical Data Rebuild.

A Canonical Data Rebuild takes the global maintenance lease, disables readiness, drains admitted mutations and worker jobs, and then replaces mutable business data in one validated transaction. Hourly fixture work pauses and performs one catch-up reconciliation afterward; overlapping rebuilds serialize, with redundant scheduled attempts coalesced. A failed rebuild retries once, then rolls back and reopens the prior verified state; it remains unavailable when rollback or health verification cannot establish safety. Indeterminate recovery preserves maintenance, assesses database reachability, schema compatibility, lease ownership, fixture generation, Audit evidence, and aggregate invariants, and then permits only a separately approved verify-and-reopen, clean Canonical Data Rebuild, or established backup-restoration action. Service returns only after canonical aggregates and ledger invariants, schema compatibility, three readiness probes, a fresh worker heartbeat, fixture reconciliation, and the deployed role smoke suite all pass. Surviving Audit partitions receive a start entry and exactly one completed, rolled-back, or indeterminate terminal entry for every rebuild attempt, including the initiator, correlation and fixture-generation identifiers, fixture-manifest and schema versions, timing, aggregate validation evidence, and a safe failure code; each state-changing recovery action records separate correlated evidence, while detailed diagnostics remain in operational telemetry.
