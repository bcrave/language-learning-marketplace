# Maintain time-relevant demo fixtures

The synthetic environment combines a canonical nightly rebuild with rolling showcase fixtures maintained hourly by the worker. Canonical data covers curriculum, roles, credits, history, reporting, capacity, waitlists, substitutions, cancellation boundaries, and daylight-saving examples. Rolling, clearly labeled sessions touch at their boundaries and keep the shared Student and Teacher identities continuously associated with a current or immediately upcoming simulated classroom. The showcase uses the real clock and ordinary scheduling invariants rather than a client-visible fake-time mode, and its transient business records disappear during the nightly rebuild. Audit partitions survive the rebuild, and the reset itself records its fixture-generation identifier.
